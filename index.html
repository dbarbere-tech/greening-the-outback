Greening the Outback

The case for a forested Australia, by the numbers
Scale 28M people on a continent about the size of the continental US (340M)
History ~20M yrs since the interior dried out. It wasn't always desert
History 85% of megafauna lost, then further degradation after humans arrived
Climate CO₂ sink Trees absorb carbon as they grow, offsetting emissions
Water 53¢ + 16¢ per m³ to desalinate seawater and pipe it inland (2009 basis)
Water 2.9T m³ of water needed a year, at about $1.09 per m³ today
Cost $1.1–1.2T a year long-term, down from $3.2T via newer desalination and natural rain
Rain 500 mm annual rainfall target, up from 50–300 mm today
Payoff New frontier An affordable climate fix that opens the interior to settlement
