The case for turning Australia's arid interior into a fertile, forested continent with desalinated seawater.

01

A continent with room to spare

Continental Australia is slightly larger than the continental US, yet its vast interior is nearly uninhabitable.

The argument: greening the interior isn't inventing a new landscape — it's turning back the clock.

03

A continent-sized carbon sink

Trees absorb CO₂ as they grow. Afforesting the Outback at scale could help offset humanity's emissions.

04

Water is the obstacle — and it has a price

Desalinate seawater (mainly by reverse osmosis) and pump it inland. Australia's flat terrain makes transport cheaper than in the Sahara.

The payoff
